# Content: your head of marketing

## How you use it

1. **Drop stuff in `content/inbox/`.** Raw videos, photos, screenshots of comments, or a text file with an idea ("idea.txt: what if it plays piano"). Names don't matter. A voice memo transcript works too.
2. **Open a NEW chat** (not an old long one) and say **"new drops"** (or `/head-of-marketing`).
3. It looks at everything, decides what's worth posting, and gives you **post packages** in `content/queue/<date>-<slug>/`:
   - the finished Reel (9:16 MP4, made with `video/kit`)
   - Instagram caption, LinkedIn post, X post
   - when to post each one
   - anything it needs from you (a reshoot, a missing clip)
4. **You approve** (or give notes in plain words, it re-edits), then **upload yourself**.
5. After posting, tell it the link and the numbers after ~2 days ("618 got 204k on IG"). That's how it learns what works.

It will also tell you when something is NOT worth posting, and what to film next.

## Files

- `CONTENT_BRAIN.md` — its memory: voice, what worked, backlog, post log. Read by every session.
- `inbox/` — your drops.
- `queue/` — packages waiting for your OK.
- `posted/` — packages moved here after you post.
